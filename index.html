SCIENTIFIC TRANSPARENCY

Clear about what computation can — and cannot — tell you.

Linkwright supports computational exploration. Sanitised structures and generated product graphs do not establish synthetic feasibility, selectivity, yield, stability, biological activity, safety or therapeutic value. Reaction conditions and candidate structures require qualified expert review and experimental validation. Docking and molecular-dynamics services will only be offered when their operation is verified for the relevant pilot environment.

05 / QUESTIONS

Before you join.

Is Linkwright a fully released commercial SaaS? +

No. Linkwright is currently being offered as a limited early-access pilot while the platform and its operational capabilities continue to be tested.

Does Linkwright guarantee that a hybrid can be synthesised? +

No. Hybrid-design outputs are computational hypotheses. A qualified chemist must assess reaction feasibility, conditions, selectivity and safety, followed by experimental validation.

Are docking and molecular dynamics included? +

Only where the relevant tools are operationally verified for the specific pilot environment and scope. Ask for a feature demonstration before agreeing to a pilot.

How does payment work? +

This website does not collect payments. Pilot scope and price are confirmed directly first; payment instructions are provided only after both parties agree.

Can my laboratory request a demonstration? +

Yes. Use the enquiry form below and describe your research area and the workflow you want to evaluate.
